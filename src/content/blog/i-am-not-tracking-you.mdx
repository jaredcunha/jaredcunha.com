**Update**

I have installed [Cabin](https://withcabin.com/) for analytics on this site. According to their website, they are a privacy-first analytics platform, they do not require cookies, and they are GDPR and CCPA compliant.

I didn’t initially have any analytics installed here, but I’m planning on publishing much more content this year and I want to see what might be resonating. This is a change, I know, and I wanted to select an ethical platform.

Given that privacy, I am still not tracking you.
